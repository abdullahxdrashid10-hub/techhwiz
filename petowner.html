🍼 Baby Stage

Puppies / Kittens

0–3 months: 4 meals/day, softened kibble or warm wet food

3–6 months: 3 meals/day, transition to dry nutrient kibble

6–12 months: 2 meals/day, portion calibrated by target adult weight

💧 Keep fresh, filtered water accessible at all times

🐕 Active Adults

Adult Nutrition

Small breeds (1–10kg): ½–1 cup balanced kibble/day

Medium breeds (10–25kg): 1–2 cups high-protein kibble/day

Large breeds (25kg+): 2–3 cups daily split across 2 meals

⚠️ Adjust caloric intake for daily exercise levels

🐈 Senior Care

Senior Companions

7+ years dogs, 11+ years cats: Senior joint-support formula

Portion Control: Reduce calories 10–15% to protect aging joints

Key Supplements: Glucosamine, Omega-3s, and gut probiotics

🩺 Consult your veterinarian for specialized diets

🎯 Step 1

Sit & Stay Foundation

Lure with a treat above the nose. When their hips touch the floor, mark "Yes!" and reward immediately. Practice in 5-minute focused bursts.

📋 4-Step Action Loop

1. Lure treat over muzzle → 2. Mark "Yes!" when seated → 3. Deliver treat within 1.5s → 4. Release with "Free!".

⚠️ Common Mistake

Repeating "Sit, Sit, Sit!" dilutes the cue. Say it once clearly, wait 3 seconds for canine cognitive processing.

⏱️ Schedule: 5 min, 3x daily 🎯 Goal: 30s solid stay
🦮 Step 2

Loose Leash Walking

Reward your pet every time they walk beside you on a loose leash. If they pull, stand still like a tree until tension releases.

🌳 The "Be A Tree" Rule

The instant the leash tightens, stop dead. Never yank back. Take 2 steps backward; when your dog returns to your side, praise and resume moving forward.

💡 The Reinforcement Zone

Deliver small pea-sized high-value treats directly by your left thigh every 4–5 steps to teach heel positioning.

⏱️ Schedule: 15 min daily walks 🎯 Gear: 6ft fixed nylon leash
🏠 Step 3

House & Den Training

Take your pet outside directly after naps, meals, and play. Create a safe, comforting crate den filled with soft bedding and chew toys.

⏰ The 15-Minute Potty Clock

Young pets must potty 10–15 min after eating, drinking, waking up, or vigorous play. Accompany them to the exact same outdoor spot.

🏡 Positive Den Association

Feed meals inside the crate with the door wide open. Never use the crate as a time-out punishment to preserve its safe haven status.

⏱️ Night Routine: Final break at 10 PM 🧼 Tip: Enzymatic cleaner
