Our Mission

Weight Management

Maintain a healthy weight through balanced portions and regular exercise. Overweight pets are at risk for diabetes, joint problems, and reduced lifespan. Weigh your pet monthly and adjust feeding accordingly.

🦷

Dental Health

Brush your pet's teeth 2–3 times per week using pet-safe toothpaste. Dental disease affects over 80% of dogs by age 3. Look out for bad breath, drooling, or reluctance to eat as warning signs.

🤧

Common Conditions

Allergies, ear infections, and skin issues are the most common reasons for vet visits. Watch for excessive scratching, red skin, or discharge. Early detection means simpler, less costly treatment.

💊

Parasite Prevention

Use monthly flea, tick, and heartworm preventatives year-round. Parasites can transmit serious diseases to both pets and humans. Keep up with deworming schedules recommended by your vet.

Sit & Stay

Hold a treat above your pet's nose. Slowly move it backward. The moment they sit, say "Sit!" and reward immediately. Practice 5-minute sessions, 3 times daily.

🦮

Leash Walking

Start indoors with short walks. Reward your pet for walking beside you without pulling. Stop moving if they pull — only resume when the leash is slack. Patience is key.

🏠

House Training

Take your pet out after meals, naps, and play. Praise immediately after they go outside. Maintain a consistent schedule — most pets learn within 4–6 months of consistent training.
